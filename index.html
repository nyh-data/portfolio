Project — 01 Financial Analytics
P&L Overview

Profit & Loss Analytics Dashboard

Automated 3-page P&L reporting system for an e-commerce seller — covering financial overview, expense breakdown, and SKU-level product performance. Connected Google Sheets to Looker Studio with 13 months of data, dynamic date filters, and color-coded alerts.

Key findings
— Revenue grew 9.6% YoY but Net Profit Margin compressed from 35% → 27.4%. Expenses rising faster than revenue.
— Shop Subsidy accounts for ~39% of revenue and 98% of variable expenses — critical concentration risk.
— SKU 4: highest revenue (20.4M), lowest COGS (19.4%), profit +27.3% MoM. Top candidate for scaling.
— SKU 9 & 10 growth trap: revenue up 99.5% & 41.9% but net profit down -41.5% & -60.1%.
→ Reallocate subsidy from SKU 9/10 to SKU 4 to recover portfolio margin.

Project — 02 Customer Analytics

Customer Insight — RFM Segmentation

Customer segmentation using the RFM model (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) across 510K active customers. Built with dbt on Databricks for data transformation and Power BI for interactive visualization — enabling targeted retention and marketing decisions.

Key findings
— 510K active customers across 651bn net revenue with 2M total transactions.
— 85.9% of customers are Lost (438K) — highest urgency segment for winback campaigns.
— At Risk group (13.35%, 68K) is the highest-priority recovery opportunity — still reachable.
— Online leads with 255K customers (50%), Offline 153K, Hybrid 102K.
— Gold tier (76K customers) shows highest avg transaction value — prioritize retention.
→ Launch targeted re-engagement for At Risk before they move to Lost.
